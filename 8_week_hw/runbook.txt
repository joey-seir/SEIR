Note: This runbook provides high-level guidance for configuring and managing an instance group, outlining the standard steps, expected behaviors, and key decision points without prescribing every exact setting. It assumes that individuals/teams will apply judgment to fill in gaps—making additional configurations or adjustments as needed based on the specific workload, environment, and operational requirements.

Go to/sign into Google Cloud Platform (https://console.cloud.google.com/welcome/)
Search Instance Group in search bar > Under “Instance groups” click on Instance groups > Click on “Create Instance Group”

Give instance group a unique name - our naming convention is Company name-Unique instance group name-year
Ex: Acme-Week8-2026
Click in “Instance template*” under Description > Click “Create a new instance template”
Give template a unique name
Scroll down to “Firewall” > select appropriate Firewall
Click “Save and Continue”
Under “Location”, if “Multiple zones” is selected and the boxes in “Zones” section under Location is checked, the instance group will manage instances across multiple zones.
Scroll down to ”Autoscaling” > Click “Configure Autoscaling” > set min and max number of instances

Scroll down to “Autohealing” > Click into “Health check” > Click “create health check” > a left panel opens
Give health check a name, make any changes needed, when done, click “Save”
Click “Create”
